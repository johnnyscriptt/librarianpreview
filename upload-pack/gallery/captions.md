# Gallery captions

Upload in numbered order and set **01** as featured.

1. **See the book before you trade**
   Every librarian's trades are written on a sheet lying on its lectern. This one sells Efficiency V, and it is on the hunt list, so it is written in green.

2. **A whole trading hall at a glance**
   Walk down your trading hall and read every librarian's offers from the walkway. You never have to click a villager or open a trade screen.

3. **Pick the books you are hunting**
   Press U to open the settings. Search for an enchantment and click it to hunt it at any level, or click again for the top level only. You can also turn the sheets off or change how far away they show.

4. **Hunted trades are written in green**
   Any trade on your hunt list is written in green, so the librarian you want is obvious the moment you walk past.

5. **Long lists scroll with the wheel**
   A levelled-up librarian has more trades than fit on the sheet. Aim at the lectern and use the mouse wheel to scroll through them; the arrows in the margin show there is more.
