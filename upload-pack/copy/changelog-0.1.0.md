### Added
- Every claimed lectern shows its librarian's trades on a sheet of paper lying on top: costs,
  an arrow and what you get, with enchanted books named by enchantment and level.
- Rerolls appear on the sheet within about a second of putting the lectern back.
- Press **U** to pick the enchantments you are hunting (any level or top level only); those
  trades are written in green.
- Long trade lists scroll with the mouse wheel while you aim at the lectern.
- Press **H** to show or hide the sheets. Range 8 to 32 blocks, 12 by default.

### Known limitations
- Librarians only.
- Prices are base prices, without your reputation or Hero of the Village discounts.
